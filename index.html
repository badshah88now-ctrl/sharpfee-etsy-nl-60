Etsy Fees in the Netherlands: What a €60 Order Really Costs

Selling handmade goods from the Netherlands on Etsy feels simple until the fees land. A €60 order does not leave you €60. Etsy takes its cut in four separate pieces, and each one is small enough to miss — together they decide whether the sale was worth it.

Start with the listing fee: $0.20 per listing, billed in US dollars, roughly €0.20 at current rates. Then the transaction fee: 6.5% of the total price including shipping, which is €3.90 on a €60 order. Then payment processing for EU sellers: 4% plus €0.30, another €2.70. Add them up and Etsy keeps about €6.80 of your €60 order, leaving you around €53.20 before the cost of the product itself, packaging, and postage.

That is before any optional extras. Etsy's Offsite Ads can take a further 12% or 15% of the sale if an ad brought the buyer, and promoted listings add their own cost per click. A seller who turns on every promotion without checking the maths can watch a €60 sale shrink fast. So when does your own store get cheaper? Shopify's Basic plan in the EU costs €32 a month, and each order costs 2% plus €0.25 through Shopify Payments — €1.45 on a €60 order. That is €5.35 less per order than Etsy's €6.80. Divide the €32 monthly fee by that €5.35 saving and the crossover sits at roughly six orders a month. Sell more than six €60 orders a month and your own store starts winning on fees alone, before you count the control over branding, customer data, and repeat buyers.

The honest answer is not "Etsy is expensive" or "Shopify is always better". It depends on volume. Under six orders a month, Etsy's per-order fees are cheaper than paying a monthly subscription. Above that, the subscription pays for itself.
